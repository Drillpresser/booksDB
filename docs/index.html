Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Your library, patrons, and loans work entirely on your device. You only need an account to share shelves, rate books, or use the shared catalog.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. This removes your account and everything linked to it from our servers.

How do I back up my library?

Go to Settings → Export library. This saves a copy of your catalog wherever you choose.

What are the "Ask Claude" features?

They're optional AI helpers that fill in missing book details and suggest classifications. They use your own Anthropic API key, which you add in Settings.
