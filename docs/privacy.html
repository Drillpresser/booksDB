Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026 · Last updated October 7, 2026

The short version

  • Your book catalog, patrons (contacts), and loans are stored only on your device.
  • An account is optional. If you sign in, we store your account details and whatever you choose to share: shelves, ratings, reviews, requests, and book details.
  • We don't run ads, use analytics or tracking SDKs, or sell or share your data with anyone for marketing.
  • You can delete your account and its data from inside the app at any time.

Who we are

BookHoarder is an app for cataloging and lending a personal book collection. It is built and run by an independent developer ("we", "us"). Questions about this policy can go to [CONTACT EMAIL].

Data that stays on your device

The following data is kept in a database on your phone. It is never sent to us:

  • Your books and copies: titles, authors, ISBNs, classifications, notes, personal ratings, and cover images (including photos you pick from your library).
  • Patrons (contacts): names, phone numbers, email addresses, and notes you enter.
  • Loan history: who has which book and when it is due.
  • Your Anthropic API key, if you add one. It is kept in the device's secure keychain.

If you use Export library, the app builds a file of this data and passes it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes from there is up to you.

If you delete the app, this data is deleted too, apart from any copy kept in your device backups.

Third-party services the app talks to

  • Open Library (Internet Archive) and Google Books: when you scan, search for, or look up a book, the ISBN or search text is sent to them to fetch book details and covers. No account information is sent. See the Internet Archive and Google privacy policies.
  • Anthropic (Claude): only if you add your own API key and use an "Ask Claude" feature. The app then sends the book's details (and, for classification suggestions, your classification scheme) straight from your device to Anthropic under your own key. This goes through your own account with Anthropic and their privacy policy applies. It does not pass through us.
  • Apple and Google sign-in: these handle authentication under their own privacy policies.
  • Supabase: hosts our database and sign-in, as described above.

The app has no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only to scan book barcodes on the device, and no images are kept or uploaded. Photos you pick as custom covers are copied into the app's own storage on your device. They are not uploaded.

Keeping and deleting your data

Account data is kept until you delete your account. To delete it, go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account, profile, ratings and reviews, shelves and the books on them, library cards, requests, and suggested edits. Book details you contributed to the shared catalog stay available to other readers, but they are no longer linked to you. Deleting your account does not touch the data stored only on your device. If you can't access the app, email us and we will delete your account for you.

Children

BookHoarder is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you think a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. You can change most of it directly in the app. For anything else, email us and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we will also announce it in the app.
